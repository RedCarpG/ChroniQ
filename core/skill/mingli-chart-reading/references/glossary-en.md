# English glosses for the Chinese terms

When answering in English, keep each term in Chinese and add the gloss in brackets the first time it appears, for example 偏财 (Indirect Wealth). The glosses below are common choices in English BaZi and Zi Wei writing; a few terms have no settled translation, so a short explanation works better there than a coined name. Western astrology (planets, signs, houses, aspects) simply uses its usual English names.

## 八字 (BaZi, Four Pillars of Destiny)

- **Pillars and stems**
  - 四柱: Four Pillars. 年柱, 月柱, 日柱, 时柱: Year, Month, Day, Hour Pillar.
  - 天干: Heavenly Stems. 地支: Earthly Branches. 干支: stem-branch pair (sexagenary cycle).
  - 藏干: Hidden Stems, the stems stored in a branch. 本气 / 中气 / 余气: main / middle / residual qi.
  - 日主 / 日元: Day Master, the self.
- **Elements**
  - 五行: Five Elements (Phases). 木 Wood, 火 Fire, 土 Earth, 金 Metal, 水 Water.
  - 生 / 克: produce / control. 得令: in season, the month supports the Day Master. 通根: rooted, the Day Master's element is hidden in a branch. 透干: shows on a stem.
  - 旺 / 弱: strong / weak. 用神: Useful God, the element the chart most needs. 喜神: favourable element. 忌神: unfavourable element.
- **十神 (Ten Gods)**
  - 比肩 Friend (Parallel), 劫财 Rob Wealth;
  - 食神 Eating God, 伤官 Hurting Officer;
  - 正财 Direct Wealth, 偏财 Indirect Wealth;
  - 正官 Direct Officer, 七杀 Seven Killings (偏官 Indirect Officer);
  - 正印 Direct Resource, 偏印 Indirect Resource (枭神).
- **Luck**
  - 大运: 10-year Luck Pillar. 起运 / 交运: start of the luck pillars / changeover.
  - 流年: Annual Pillar. 流月: Monthly Pillar. 流日: Daily Pillar.
  - 太岁: Grand Duke of the year. 本命年: one's own zodiac year. 冲太岁: clashing the year's Grand Duke.
- **Interactions**
  - 合: combination. 六合: six harmonies. 三合: three harmony frame. 三会: directional combination.
  - 冲: clash. 刑: punishment. 害: harm. 破: destruction.
- **Others**
  - 纳音: Na Yin, the "sound" element of a pillar.
  - 十二长生: twelve life stages. 星运 / 自坐: the Day Master's / the stem's own stage on a branch.
  - 空亡: Void (Death and Emptiness). 神煞: Symbolic Stars. 天乙贵人: Nobleman (Heavenly Noble). 驿马: Travelling Horse. 桃花: Peach Blossom. 华盖: Canopy (Elegant Seal).
  - 胎元: Conception Pillar. 命宫 (八字): Life Palace pillar.

## 紫微斗数 (Zi Wei Dou Shu, Purple Star Astrology)

- **Palaces**
  - 命宫: Life Palace. 身宫: Body Palace.
  - 兄弟: Siblings. 夫妻: Spouse. 子女: Children. 财帛: Wealth. 疾厄: Health. 迁移: Travel. 仆役 / 交友: Friends. 官禄: Career. 田宅: Property. 福德: Fortune (Wellbeing). 父母: Parents.
  - 三方四正: the palace with its opposite and two trine palaces. 对宫: opposite palace. 空宫: empty palace, no major star.
- **Stars and brightness**
  - 主星: major stars, such as 紫微 Emperor, 天机, 太阳 Sun, 武曲, 天同, 廉贞, 天府, 太阴 Moon, 贪狼, 巨门, 天相, 天梁, 七杀, 破军. Names are usually left in pinyin (Tian Ji, Wu Qu …) with a word on their nature.
  - 辅星: minor stars. 杂曜: miscellaneous stars.
  - 庙旺得利平不陷: brightness from exalted to fallen.
- **Transformations and periods**
  - 四化: Four Transformations. 化禄 Prosperity, 化权 Power, 化科 Fame (Merit), 化忌 Obstacle (Taboo). 生年四化: birth-year transformations. 宫干飞化: flying transformations from a palace's stem. 自化: self-transformation.
  - 大限: 10-year Decade Limit. 流年: annual chart. 流月, 流日: monthly, daily. 运曜 / 流耀: period stars such as 运禄, 流羊.
  - 五行局: Five-Element Bureau. 命主 / 身主: Life Lord / Body Lord. 格局: pattern (configuration).

## 七政四余 (Seven Governors and Four Remainders)

- 七政: the Seven Governors, the Sun, Moon and five planets. Their old names are 辰星 Mercury, 太白 Venus, 荧惑 Mars, 岁星 Jupiter, 镇星 Saturn.
- 四余: the Four Remainders. 罗睺 Rahu and 计都 Ketu, the lunar nodes; 月孛 Yue Bei, the lunar apogee; 紫炁 Zi Qi, a reckoned point.
- 二十八宿: the 28 Lunar Mansions. 宿度 / 古度: degrees within a mansion, in ancient degrees (365¼ to the circle).
- 十二次: the twelve Jupiter Stations. 命度: the mansion of the ascendant.
- 日躔 / 月离: the Sun's / Moon's mansion. 守命: occupying the Life Palace.
- 四象: the Four Symbols. 东方青龙 Azure Dragon (east), 北方玄武 Black Tortoise (north), 西方白虎 White Tiger (west), 南方朱雀 Vermilion Bird (south).

## 黄历 (Chinese almanac)

- 宜 / 忌: auspicious / inauspicious activities. 建除十二神: Twelve Day Officers, such as 建 Establish, 除 Remove, 满 Full …
- 值神: day guardian, 黄道 auspicious or 黑道 inauspicious. 吉神 / 凶煞: auspicious / malefic spirits.
- 节气: solar term. 节: the term that begins a month. 农历: lunar calendar.
